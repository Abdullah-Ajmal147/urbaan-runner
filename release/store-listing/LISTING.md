**Full description (4000 max):**
The night market of Lantern City is open, and parcels need delivering before dawn.

Swipe to switch lanes, jump over carts, slide under awnings and run across rooftops in an endless runner made for quick, satisfying sessions.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Fast, fair runs: every obstacle can be dodged, and the city keeps getting quicker.
• Lantern Hunt: one lantern glows somewhere in every run. Find all five today for a big spark bonus.
• Daily Run: the same track for everyone, every day.
• Missions that refresh as you play, plus a daily gift streak.
• Couriers and trails to unlock in the Locker, and power-ups to upgrade: magnet, shield and double sparks.
• A short tutorial that teaches the moves in under a minute.
• Plays offline. Progress is saved on your phone and backed up to the cloud.

Free to play, with optional ads you choose to watch (for example to keep running after a crash) and an optional purchase to remove ads.

Urban Runner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Urban Runner ("the game") is published by botcraftstudio.si ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

What we collect

  • Game progress: sparks, level, unlocked items, best distance, missions, streaks and settings. It is stored on your device and backed up to Google Firebase under an anonymous account ID, so it isn't linked to your name or email.
  • Gameplay analytics: events such as runs started and ended, missions completed and ads watched, sent to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can improve the game. You can turn this off in Settings → Share usage data.
  • Crash reports: device model, Android version and technical details of a crash, sent to Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising: the game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location (from your IP address) to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options (shown in the regions where consent is required).
  • Purchases: these are handled by Google Play. We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment details.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't sell your personal data.

Third parties

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), Google AdMob and Google Play Billing process data under Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Keeping and deleting data

Uninstalling the game deletes the progress on your device. To delete the cloud backup, email us at the address below and we'll remove it within 30 days.
